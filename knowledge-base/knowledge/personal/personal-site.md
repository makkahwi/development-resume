# Personal 3D Website

Suhaib has a personal 3D website focused on non-professional aspects of his life.

Its purpose is to present hobbies, places, media, and everyday interests through a personal 3D environment rather than functioning as a professional résumé.

The site's personal identity concept is organized around three broad areas:

- Study
- Career
- Stays

Each area highlights three important choices or phases, creating a 'three aspects, three choices each' identity concept.
