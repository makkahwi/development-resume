Why do so many MVPs get rebuilt after the first year? And why is that not always a bad thing?

Rebuilding a product is not always a sign of failure. In many cases, it’s the first truly mature decision a team makes.

## The uncomfortable truth

The first version of almost any product is built on assumptions, expectations, and incomplete understanding of real users.

## When rebuilding is the right decision

- You now have real usage data

- You understand your users better

- You know what must stay and what must go

- The rebuild solves core problems
